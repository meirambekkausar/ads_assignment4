Task 1: Depth First Search (DFS) Trace
Source Node: A
Adjacency Lists:
A: C, B, D
B: A, C, E, G
C: A, B, D
D: C, A
E: G, F, B
F: G, E
G: F, B

DFS Step-by-Step Trace:
1. Start at A. Visit A. (Visited: A)
2. Move to first unvisited neighbor of A: C. (Visited: A, C)
3. From C, move to first unvisited neighbor: B. (Visited: A, C, B)
4. From B, move to first unvisited neighbor: E. (Visited: A, C, B, E)
5. From E, move to first unvisited neighbor: G. (Visited: A, C, B, E, G)
6. From G, move to first unvisited neighbor: F. (Visited: A, C, B, E, G, F)
7. From F, neighbors G and E are already visited. Backtrack to G.
8. From G, neighbors F and B are already visited. Backtrack to E.
9. From E, backtrack to B.
10. From B, backtrack to C.
11. From C, visit next unvisited neighbor: D. (Visited: A, C, B, E, G, F, D)
12. Backtrack from D to C, then back to A.
Final DFS Order: A, C, B, E, G, F, D


Task 2: Breadth First Search (BFS) Trace
Source Node: A

BFS Step-by-Step Trace (Queue):
1. Enqueue A. (Queue: [A], Visited: A)
2. Dequeue A. Enqueue neighbors C, B, D. (Queue: [C, B, D], Visited: A, C, B, D)
3. Dequeue C. Neighbors A, B, D are already visited/queued. (Queue: [B, D])
4. Dequeue B. Enqueue unvisited neighbors E, G. (Queue: [D, E, G], Visited: A, C, B, D, E, G)
5. Dequeue D. Neighbors C, A already visited. (Queue: [E, G])
6. Dequeue E. Enqueue unvisited neighbor F. (Queue: [G, F], Visited: A, C, B, D, E, G, F)
7. Dequeue G. All neighbors visited. (Queue: [F])
8. Dequeue F. All neighbors visited. (Queue: [])
Final BFS Order: A, C, B, D, E, G, F
